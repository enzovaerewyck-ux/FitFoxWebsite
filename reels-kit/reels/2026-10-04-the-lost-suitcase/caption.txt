Animated fox Vex arrives in a European city but his suitcase never shows up. With dinner at 8 PM and only a small carry-on, he tries a few looks, asks the FitFox app to use only what he has, builds an outfit from a navy knit polo, his trousers, an olive overshirt and white sneakers, and gets complimented at dinner. That night his overflowing suitcase arrives.
